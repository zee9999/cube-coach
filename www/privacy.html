Cube Coach privacy policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

This policy covers the Cube Coach app for Android and iPhone and the Cube Coach website. Cube Coach has no accounts: we never ask for your name, email or phone number, and we do not sell personal data.

Camera

Cube Coach uses your camera only to read the sticker colors of your cube. Images are processed on your device and are never uploaded, stored on a server or shared.

Data stored on your device

Your coins, unlocked skins, best times and finished lessons are saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show, measure and protect ads, the AdMob SDK collects your IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), your device’s advertising ID, ad interactions such as views and taps, and diagnostic data such as app performance. This data is sent to Google and may be shared with Google’s advertising partners. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases (Google Play, App Store and RevenueCat)

Payments are handled by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details. RevenueCat records which items your store account bought, linked to a random ID, so your purchases can be unlocked and restored. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Your choices

  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalized ads. Change your choice any time in Shop › Ad privacy choices.
  • On Android, reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings › Privacy › Ads. On iPhone, you are asked before any tracking and can change it in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Buying Pro removes ads, so AdMob no longer runs.

Security

All data the app sends is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Keeping and deleting data

Data on your device stays until you delete the app. Purchase records are kept while they are needed to restore your purchases. To have your purchase record deleted, contact us with your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.) or App Store order ID. Google keeps ad data under its own retention policy.

Children

Cube Coach is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/zee9999/cube-coach/issues.
