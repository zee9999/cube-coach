Cube Coach privacy policy

Last updated 17 October 2026

Camera

Cube Coach uses your camera only to read the sticker colors of your cube. Images are processed on your device and are never uploaded, stored on a server or shared.

Data stored on your device

Your coins, unlocked skins, best times and finished lessons are saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) through RevenueCat, which records which items your store account owns so they can be restored. We never see your payment details.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use device identifiers to show and measure ads, subject to your device’s tracking settings. On iPhone you are asked before any tracking. Buying Pro removes ads.

Children

Cube Coach is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/zee9999/cube-coach/issues.
