Cube Coach privacy policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

This policy covers the Cube Coach app for Android and iPhone and the Cube Coach website. Cube Coach has no accounts: we never ask for your name, email or phone number, and we do not sell personal data.

Who is responsible

Cube Coach is made by Zeyad Sherif Ibrahim Noureldin, Cairo, Egypt, who is the controller of the personal data described here. For privacy questions, requests about your data or deletion requests, email zee9x9@gmail.com.

Camera

Cube Coach uses your camera only to read the sticker colors of your cube. Images are processed on your device and are never uploaded, stored on a server or shared.

Data stored on your device

Your coins, unlocked skins, best times and finished lessons are saved on your device. They are not sent to us. If your phone backs up app data to your Google or Apple account, this data can be included in that backup and restored on a new phone. Deleting the app removes it from the device.

What is collected, why, and on what legal basis

  • Ads (Google AdMob). The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show, measure and protect ads, the AdMob SDK collects your IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), your device’s advertising ID, ad interactions such as views and taps, and diagnostic data such as app performance. Google receives this data as an independent controller and may share it with its advertising partners. Personalized ads are shown only with your consent where the law requires it (consent, GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). Non-personalized or limited ads, ad measurement and fraud prevention rely on our legitimate interest in funding the free app (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Purchases (Google Play, App Store and RevenueCat). Payments are handled by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details. RevenueCat processes, on our behalf, which items your store account bought (product, date and store order number), linked to a random ID, so purchases can be unlocked and restored. Legal basis: providing what you bought (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). See the RevenueCat privacy policy.
  • Age signals (Android). Where the law requires it, for example in Brazil and some U.S. states, the app asks Google Play for your age range, and Google Play may ask you or your parent whether to share it. If Google Play reports that you are under 18, or that your age still has to be verified where the law requires it, the app turns ads off. The result (such as “under 18” or “adult”) is kept in the app’s data on your device, which can be included in your phone’s backup. It is never sent to us, to Google’s ad services or to anyone else, and it is not used for advertising, analytics or profiling. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in protecting minors and meeting the laws of the countries where the app is available (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Fonts (Google Fonts). The app and website load their fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when they load. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in showing the app correctly (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Website hosting (GitHub Pages). GitHub hosts the website and records visitors’ IP addresses in its server logs for security. Legal basis: legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Emails you send us. Used only to answer you. Legal basis: legitimate interest in answering your request (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

Ad and purchase data are needed to use the free version and to buy items. You can avoid ad data by buying Pro, which stops AdMob from running. We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing. If you consent to personalized ads, Google uses your data to build an ad profile.

International transfers

Google, RevenueCat and GitHub process data in the United States and other countries. Transfers from the EEA, UK and Switzerland rely on safeguards such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long data is kept

  • Data on your device stays until you delete the app.
  • Purchase records are kept while they are needed to restore your purchases and for the stores’ and our tax and accounting records.
  • Emails are kept only as long as needed to answer them.
  • Google keeps ad data under its own retention policy.

Your rights and choices

  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalized ads. Change or withdraw your choice any time in Shop › Ad privacy choices. Withdrawing does not affect what happened before.
  • On Android, reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings › Privacy › Ads. On iPhone, you are asked before any tracking and can change it in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or some U.S. states), you can ask for access to, correction, deletion or a copy of your data, restrict or object to its use, and opt out of the sale or sharing of data for targeted ads. Email us and we answer within one month.
  • We hold no ad data that identifies you. To find your purchase record, include your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.) or App Store order ID in your email. Please don’t post order numbers publicly.
  • You can complain to the data protection authority where you live.

Delete your data

To have your data deleted:

  1. Email zee9x9@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Cube Coach data”.
  2. If you bought anything, include your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.) so we can find your purchase record.
  3. We delete it within one month and email you to confirm.

What is deleted: your purchase record at RevenueCat and our emails with you. What is kept: Google Play’s own order records, which Google keeps under its policies, and the records tax law requires us to keep (order numbers and amounts, for the required period). Data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. To remove ad data, reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings.

Security

All data the app sends is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Children

Cube Coach is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child under 13 has shared data with us, email us and we will delete it. When Google Play tells the app a user is under 18, the app turns ads off for them (see Age signals above).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Privacy questions and requests: zee9x9@gmail.com. Bug reports can also go to github.com/zee9999/cube-coach/issues (public, so don’t include personal details there).
